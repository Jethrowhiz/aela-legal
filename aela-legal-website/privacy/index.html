Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jethro Samson (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use Aela, a personal desktop assistant that may connect to third-party services such as TikTok.

1. Information we may process

  • Information you provide: Prompts, settings, files, text, and other information you choose to submit to Aela.
  • Device and diagnostic information: Application version, error logs, and technical details needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application, if collected by your build.
  • Connected TikTok information: Account and profile details, videos, and other data available through the TikTok permissions you authorize.
  • Authorization data: Access tokens, refresh tokens, and related OAuth security data required to maintain an authorized connection.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide Aela’s features, respond to your instructions, maintain your TikTok connection, perform actions you authorize, display information returned by TikTok, troubleshoot errors, protect the application, and comply with legal obligations.

3. TikTok data and permissions

When you connect TikTok, authorization takes place through TikTok’s official authorization flow. Aela requests only the permissions configured for the application and shown during consent. The information Aela can retrieve or actions it can perform depend on TikTok’s API availability, your consent, and any required developer approval.

TikTok data obtained through its APIs is used only to provide the features you request and as permitted by TikTok’s applicable developer terms and policies. Aela does not ask you to provide your TikTok password directly to the application.

4. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through Aela. Authorization tokens should be stored securely and should not be exposed in application logs, public repositories, or client-side code. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate features you request, with third-party platforms when you authorize an integration, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Aela may use third-party AI, hosting, or other technology providers to deliver its features. Information processed by those providers is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. The providers actually used by your installation should be identified where relevant.

6. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Retention depends on Aela's actual storage and logging configuration. You can disconnect TikTok to stop future access through the integration. Disconnecting does not automatically delete information already stored locally or by TikTok.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to connect a third-party account, disconnect an existing connection, and stop using Aela. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information.

You may also revoke Aela's authorization through your TikTok account settings. For privacy requests, contact jethrosamson13@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

Aela is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the application or the connected third-party services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. International processing

Depending on the services and infrastructure used by your installation, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Aela's features or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated.
