Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aela, a personal AI-powered desktop assistant developed under the JETHRO personal technology brand, may handle information when you use it. The operator is Jethro Samson (“JETHRO,” “we,” or “us”), based in Jalingo, Taraba State, Nigeria. This is a personal brand, not a representation that JETHRO is a registered company.

Implementation review notice: The Aela application source code was not available for inspection when this policy was prepared. Accordingly, the exact information collected, processing locations, providers, token storage, security controls, retention periods, and deletion behavior have not been independently verified. The descriptions below distinguish possible processing from verified implementation facts. The operator should confirm and update these details against the released application before relying on this policy as a complete description of actual practices.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use, the permissions you grant, and the application's implementation, Aela may process:

  • Voice recordings, speech transcriptions, or other voice input when you use voice features.
  • Text prompts, commands, and information you choose to provide to Aela.
  • Information needed to perform desktop productivity or computer-assistance tasks.
  • Information returned by a connected platform, such as authorized TikTok profile details, video information, or performance information available through approved API permissions.
  • Content and files you select for management or publishing features.
  • Technical or diagnostic information, if the application implements collection of such information.

The precise categories depend on the enabled feature and actual implementation. Do not provide sensitive information in a voice or text prompt unless it is necessary for the task and you are comfortable with the applicable processing.

2. Voice and text input

Voice input may be captured or processed to recognize commands and provide requested assistance. Text input may be processed to interpret and carry out your requests. Whether audio is transcribed on your device or sent to a service provider, whether raw audio or transcripts are retained, and for how long are implementation details that have not been verified. Please confirm these details before publication and before using voice features with sensitive information.

3. TikTok account information

If TikTok integration is available and you choose to connect an account, Aela uses TikTok's official authorization and API systems. You must authorize access before Aela can access information through that integration. Aela does not ask you to provide your TikTok password directly to Aela.

The information Aela can access depends on the approved API scopes, the permissions you grant, and TikTok's policies and technical limits. Profile information, video management, uploads, publishing, and performance information may be limited or unavailable. No unapproved access or capability is promised. TikTok operates independently under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. OAuth authorization and access tokens

When you authorize a connected account, the authorization process may provide Aela with access credentials, such as access tokens, needed to make authorized API requests. The exact token types, where and how they are stored, who can access them, whether they are transmitted to another service, and how they are deleted or invalidated depend on the implementation and have not been verified. These details must be reviewed against the application source and deployment configuration before this policy is finalized.

5. How information may be used

Information may be processed to interpret and carry out commands you submit, provide desktop assistance and productivity features, connect to accounts you authorize, retrieve or manage information available through approved APIs, perform content actions you request, maintain functionality and security, troubleshoot problems, and respond to privacy or support inquiries. Actual uses depend on which features are implemented and enabled.

6. Storage and security

Aela may process information locally on your device and/or through remote services, depending on its implementation. The actual storage locations, data flows, security safeguards, encryption practices, and access controls have not been established from source code available for review. We therefore do not claim a particular storage provider, encryption method, or security certification. The operator must verify and document these arrangements before publication. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. AI, voice, cloud, and other providers

Aela is described as AI-powered, but the AI, speech recognition, cloud, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, and storage providers used by the application—and the information each receives—could not be confirmed from available source code. Where an external provider processes your input, that provider may receive the information needed to deliver the relevant feature and may process it under its own terms and privacy practices. The operator must identify the actual providers and their purposes, and verify their retention and data-use terms, before this policy is treated as complete.

8. Information sharing

Information may be sent to a third party when necessary to provide a feature you request, including an authorized platform or a provider actually used by Aela. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the application, or legal rights. The application’s actual recipients and data-sharing flows have not been verified; no claim is made here that a particular provider receives—or does not receive—specific data. JETHRO does not claim to sell personal information.

9. Data retention

Specific retention periods and deletion behavior have not been verified. Information should be retained only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was processed, subject to applicable legal requirements. The actual retention periods for prompts, audio, transcripts, platform data, tokens, diagnostics, and backups must be confirmed from the implementation and any provider settings; this policy does not promise a fixed period or automatic deletion.

10. Disconnecting an account

You may revoke TikTok authorization using applicable TikTok account or authorization settings. Revoking access should prevent future API access that depends on that authorization, subject to TikTok's systems, but it may not automatically erase information already stored by Aela or a provider. The application’s account-disconnection controls and their effect on stored data have not been verified.

11. Data deletion requests

You may submit a request to ask what personal information is handled or to request deletion by emailing jethrosamson13@gmail.com. Requests will be considered under applicable law. The ability to locate and delete data from the application, connected providers, and backups, and the time required to do so, depend on the actual implementation and have not been verified. We do not promise deletion from systems that cannot be identified or controlled; any applicable legal retention obligations may also apply.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request and may be unable to fulfill it in full where an exception under applicable law applies.

13. Children's privacy

Aela is not intended for use by children who are below the minimum age required by applicable law or the terms of a connected platform. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information unlawfully. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Aela, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

14. International processing and third-party services

Because Aela may interact with online platforms or external providers, information may be processed in locations outside your own. The actual providers and locations have not been verified. Third-party services, including TikTok, operate independently and are governed by their own privacy notices and terms. Review those materials before authorizing an integration.

15. Policy updates

This Policy may be updated as Aela's implementation, features, providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its effective date. Please review it periodically.

16. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Jethro Samson, operating under the JETHRO personal technology brand, at jethrosamson13@gmail.com.
